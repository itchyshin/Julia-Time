9:00 am · Itchy’s lab · Chapter 1

Toto's report says the springtails are dying out. Are they?

Toto rushes in with a one-page report on batch B09. Springtails, tiny relatives of the snow fleas seen on winter snow, are jumping animals that the lab rears in closed jars, on damp leaf litter, for its experiments. A batch is a set of jars started on the same day. B09 is six jars, two per tray on three trays, started six weeks ago with about twenty springtails each. Each lid has a fine mesh for air; springtails cannot get in or out. After six weeks the live ones have bred and the dead ones have rotted away, so nobody knows how many are in a jar; the look only asks whether any live ones are there.

“Report, batch B09. Tray T-C: 0 jars with springtails. Conclusion: the springtails are dying out.”

Dying out here means jars going empty.

Six weeks on, the three B09 trays were checked one tray each evening: T-A first, then T-B, then T-C. Each jar got a two-minute look and one row in the notebook: springtails seen, or not seen. Seen means at least one live, moving springtail turned up during the look; nobody counts them. Toto typed the tally sheet into the lab’s table, then read that table as T-A: 2 of 2 jars, T-B: 2 of 2 jars, then T-C: 0 jars.

Toto: “Both jars, both jars, then none. They are dying out!”

Momo: “One zero in a report is a weak reason to believe that.”

Itchy: “Before we believe it, look at the notebook. Each row was written while someone looked at the jar.”

The notebook has one row per jar. It may tell a different story from the report. You are the lab’s newest investigator.

Your first job: find the B09 jars in the notebook.

    One row, one jar

    Two short warm-ups on practice rows, so picking the B09 jars is not your first try.

    A table has rows and columns. Each row is one jar; each column is one detail. Julia selects them in this order: rows first, columns second.

    jars[rows, columns]

    Try the first three rows: type jars[1:3, :]. The range 1:3 means rows 1 through 3; the final : means all columns. These are practice rows, not necessarily batch B09.
