Chapter 4 · Plan a fair recheck

The report was wrong. Is the notebook right?

T-C has springtails after all: the notebook shows springtails in 1 of its 2 jars, and the report’s 0 was a blank box. Toto meant the springtails are dying out, tray after tray, but three trays of two jars are too few to show that. What we can test is simpler: if the springtails were dying out, most jars would have none. The notebook says 5 of the 6 B09 jars have springtails, so everything rests on one question: is the notebook right?

A two-minute look can miss springtails hiding deep in the leaves, so “not seen” is not always “none there”. A slow, leaf-by-leaf look will tell. The lab takes two jars from every batch for a routine DNA species check. J-093 and J-095 were checked with their trays, then went to the freezer the next day. So four B09 jars can still be looked at, and T-C now has only J-096 left to recheck. A slow look takes an afternoon per jar, and there are three afternoons: time for three of the four jars.

Itchy: “If we choose the three, we will pick the jars we already trust. Let chance pick, and pick fairly.”

Chance picks them, so nobody picks favourites; chance may even leave out J-096, and that is the price of a fair test.
