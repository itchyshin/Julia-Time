Julia Time

Learn Julia by solving one lab mystery: The Case of the Missing Fleas.

Toto says the lab's springtails are dying out. The notebook may say otherwise. You find out by typing short lines of Julia, one at a time, and reading what comes back.

  • For: graduate students and researchers. Never coded: start at Step 1. Know R or Python: same path; skip any round you already know.
  • Time: about 4 hours in six steps. Stop any time; your place is saved here.
  • Needs: Julia and the Julia Time launcher, set up once. Nothing is scored or sent anywhere.

Each step is a practice Lesson, then a Chapter on the real case. In each one you read a short question, type a line of Julia and press Run.
